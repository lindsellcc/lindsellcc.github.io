About Lindsell CC

Friendly village cricket in North Essex.

Lindsell Cricket Club is a friendly village club based in North Essex. Match days combine competitive cricket with a relaxed social atmosphere, with fixtures, club events and touring sides forming the heart of the season.

This first version of the website gives the club a single home for cricket information, live scoring, club news, sponsors, contact details and links to our existing Play-Cricket and Square services.
